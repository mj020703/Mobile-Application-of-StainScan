Objectives of the System
The main objective of the system is to develop a reliable, intelligent, and userfriendly mobile application called StainScan that uses Convolutional Neural Networks
(CNNs) and image recognition technology to identify common stains on cotton fabrics and
provide safe, effective, and accurate stain-removal recommendations. 

Specific Objectives of the System
1. To develop a mobile application featuring a user-friendly interface that allows users to
upload images of stained cotton fabrics for identification and cleaning assistance. 2. To implement and evaluate a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)-based image
recognition model to accurately classify common fabric stains such as:
a. used cooking oil, b.black ballpoint pen ink and;
c. mud
3. To provide appropriate stain-removal recommendations based on the identified stain
type to guide users toward safer, more informed cleaning decisions. 4. To prevent fabric damage, discoloration, and permanent staining by helping users avoid
improper or ineffective cleaning methods. 5. To optimize cleaning efficiency by minimizing the waste of water, detergent, time, and
manual effort (such as excessive scrubbing or repeated washing).To modernize
traditional stain-removal practices through the integration of image recognition
and machine learning technologies in consumer-level fabric care.

Scope and Limitations of the System
This system focuses on the design and development of StainScan, a mobile
application that uses Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and image recognition
technology to identify stains from uploaded images of cotton fabrics. The system is
intended for households, laundry workers, students, and general users who need guidance
in stain removal. The study covers the development period for Academic Year 2025–2026
STI West Negros University 9
The main features of the proposed system includes user registration and login, image upload, stain classification, scan history, and recommendation of stain-removal
procedures based on the identified stain type. The system is limited to selected stain categories only, specifically; used cooking
oil, black ballpoint pen ink, and mud. It is also limited to cotton fabric only. The accuracy
of the system may be affected by image quality, lighting conditions, camera resolution, stain visibility, and similarity between stain appearances. The system does not guarantee
complete stain removal, as actual results may vary depending on stain age, amount of stain, cleaning materials used, and user application of the recommended procedure.
